# std-bits/excel

`Standard.Excel`'s Java: `org.enso.excel` (`ExcelReader`, `ExcelWriter`,
`ExistingDataMode`, the sheet and workbook readers for `.xlsx`, `.xls` and
`.xlsb`, `ExcelRange`, the connection pool in `internal`, the streaming
`xssfreader`) packaged as `std-excel.jar` in the library's `polyglot/java`
together with Apache POI (through the `poi-wrapper` assembly), `xmlbeans` and
`log4j`. `std-base` and `std-table` are `provided`. The format itself is Enso,
in `distribution/lib/Standard/Excel/`.
