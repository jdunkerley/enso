# std-bits/fixed-width

`Standard.Fixed_Width`'s Java: `org.enso.fixed_width.FixedWidthReader`, its
problem aggregators and exceptions, and the `InvalidFixedWidthRow` and
`InconsistentFixedWidthLengths` problems, packaged as `std-fixed-width.jar` in
the library's `polyglot/java`. `std-base` and `std-table` are `provided`. The
format itself is Enso, in `distribution/lib/Standard/Fixed_Width/`.
